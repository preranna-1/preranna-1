## 🌻 what i do

> I design things. I work with data. Sometimes both at once — and I love it.

- 🎨 **frontend design** — I build clean, aesthetic interfaces from scratch. HTML & CSS, carefully crafted.
- 🤖 **ML & image models** — trained image classification models, built CNNs, made sense of messy real-world data.
- 📊 **data work** — cleaning, analysing, visualising. I genuinely enjoy the process of finding meaning in numbers.
- 🔧 **backend systems** — designed and built backends end-to-end. databases, APIs, the whole pipeline.
